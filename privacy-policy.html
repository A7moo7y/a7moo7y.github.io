Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Halloween 2048 ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Halloween 2048 does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account creation, logins, or any form of registration.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device using Android's DataStore:

• Your current game board state (to enable "Continue Game")
• Your best score and score history
• Whether you have completed the first-launch tutorial

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, including:

• Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
• General device and network information
• App interaction data for ad performance measurement

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policies. You can review Google's privacy policy at: policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. No Third-Party Analytics

The App does not integrate any analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar). We do not track your gameplay behavior, sessions, or in-app actions beyond what AdMob requires for ad delivery.

5. Children's Privacy

Halloween 2048 is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Internet Permission

The App requests internet access solely for the purpose of loading and displaying advertisements via Google AdMob. No other network communication occurs.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
