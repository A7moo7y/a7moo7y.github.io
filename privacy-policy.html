Privacy Policy

DevSparkCreations — Mobile Apps & Games

Last updated: August 10, 2026
📱 This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications and games developed and published by DevSparkCreations on Google Play and other app stores, unless a specific app provides its own separate policy.

1. Who We Are

We are an independent mobile developer publishing apps and games under the name DevSparkCreations. Our apps are available on the Google Play Store. You can contact us at: DevSparkCreations@gmail.com

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Our apps are designed with privacy in mind. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information (PII)
  • Require account registration or login
  • Access your contacts, camera, microphone, or files (unless explicitly stated for a specific feature)
  • Share any data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Sell your data — ever

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Some of our apps save game progress, settings, or preferences directly on your device only. This may include:

  • Game scores, levels, and progress
  • User preferences and settings
  • Tutorial completion state

This data is stored using Android's standard local storage (SharedPreferences or DataStore) and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party. It is fully deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising — Google AdMob

Some of our apps display advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may automatically collect certain information to deliver and measure ads, including:

  • Android Advertising ID (AAID)
  • General device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Network/connection type
  • General location (country/region level — not precise GPS)
  • App interaction signals for ad performance measurement

This data is collected and processed by Google under their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Opt-out: You can limit ad tracking at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (Android 12+) or Opt out of Ads Personalization (older versions).

5. In-App Purchases

Some apps may offer optional in-app purchases. All payment processing is handled entirely by the Google Play Billing system. We do not collect, store, or process any payment card information. Google's payment privacy policy applies to all transactions.

6. Internet Permission

Apps that request internet access do so solely for one or more of the following legitimate purposes:

  • Loading and displaying advertisements (AdMob)
  • Submitting or retrieving leaderboard scores (if applicable)
  • Downloading app updates or additional content (if applicable)

No personal data is transmitted over the internet by our apps.

7. Analytics

Unless explicitly stated in an individual app's description, our apps do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.). We do not track gameplay sessions, user funnels, or behavioral patterns.

8. Children's Privacy

Our apps are generally not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information through one of our apps, please contact us at DevSparkCreations@gmail.com and we will promptly remove it.

Apps specifically designed for children will clearly indicate this and will comply with applicable laws including COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy.

9. Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal data, the risk surface is minimal. For any data stored locally on your device, Android's standard OS-level security protections apply. We recommend keeping your device's OS and our apps updated.

10. Third-Party Links

Some apps may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before interacting with them.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our apps evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our apps after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will update the app listing on the Play Store.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have rights regarding your data under laws such as GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California). Since we do not collect personal data, there is typically nothing to access, correct, or delete. However, if you have any concerns, please contact us and we will respond promptly.
